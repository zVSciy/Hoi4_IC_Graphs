# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HOI4 IC Analyzer to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone.

Hearts of Iron IV is a game about the Second World War. Discussing its history and mechanics is
on topic. Promoting or glorifying the ideologies, regimes and crimes of that era is not.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- assuming good intent and asking before judging,
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- insults, personal or political attacks, trolling and derogatory comments,
- harassment of any kind, in public or in private,
- hate speech, extremist symbols or propaganda, including "ironic" use,
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances,
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission,
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, **zVSciy**, privately, through the contact
options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/zVSciy). All reports are reviewed promptly
and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Depending on severity, responses
range from a private note explaining the problem to a permanent ban from the project.
